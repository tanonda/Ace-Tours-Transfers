# Reference — Reviews Moderation

**Path:** Admin → Reviews (or Admin → Reviews & Ratings)

The Reviews screen is used to moderate guest-submitted reviews and ratings before they are shown publicly on the website.

---

## Metrics & Distribution

- **Filter Buttons:**
  - **All** — Total count of all reviews in the system.
  - **Pending** — Reviews awaiting approval (default view).
  - **Approved** — Reviews currently displayed on the website.
  - **Rejected** — Hidden/rejected reviews.
- **Avg Rating:** The current global star average score.
- **Rating Distribution:** Breakdown of the percentages and total counts of reviews from 1 to 5 stars.

---

## Moderation Tools

- **Search box:** Filter reviews using customer name, tour/product title, comment text, or star rating.
- **Filters:** Dropdown filters for specific Star Ratings (1-5) and Categories (Tours vs. Transfers).
- **Bulk Actions:** Select multiple rows to **Approve All**, **Reject All**, or **Delete All** permanently.

---

## Moderation Process

1. **Review Details:** Click on a review row to open the full comment details, submitter metadata, and creation date.
2. **Approve:** Click the green check icon to approve. Approved reviews display a green badge and go live instantly.
3. **Reject:** Click the red cross icon to reject. Rejected reviews are hidden from the website but stored in the system.
4. **Delete:** Click the trash icon to permanently remove the review record from the system.
